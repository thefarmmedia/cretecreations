## Cannibalization found & fixed

Four pages from the site's older "landing page" set were built with titles and H1s nearly identical to the main nav-linked service pages, meaning two pages on the site were competing for the exact same query. None of the four had any internal links pointing to them (they were orphans, reachable only via the sitemap), so the fix was to give each one a distinct, legitimate angle rather than delete or redirect a substantial page:

| Page | Was competing with | Old title | New angle | New title |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/metallic-epoxy-joplin` | `/metallic-epoxy` | "Metallic Epoxy Floors Joplin MO \| Artisan Pours" | Cost & process (the page already had real $/sq ft pricing) | "Metallic Epoxy Floor Cost Joplin MO \| Pricing & Process" |
| `/garage-floor-coating-joplin-mo` | `/epoxy-floors` | "Garage Floor Coating Joplin MO \| Epoxy & Polyaspartic" | Options comparison (epoxy vs. polyaspartic vs. box-store paint/DIY kits) | "Garage Floor Coating Options in Joplin MO: Epoxy vs Polyaspartic vs Paint" |
| `/driveway-overlay-joplin` | `/driveways` | "Driveway Overlay Joplin MO \| 11 Styles" | Overlay-vs-replacement cost decision (page already led with the $8,000+ replacement comparison) | "Driveway Overlay vs Replacement Cost Joplin MO" |
| `/epoxy-garage-floor-cost-joplin` | `/blog-cost-guide-joplin` | "Epoxy Garage Floor Cost Joplin MO — 2025 Pricing Guide" | Quick price-by-garage-size chart vs. the blog's full educational guide (already linked to the blog post — sharpened the split) | "Epoxy Garage Floor Cost by Garage Size \| Joplin MO Price Chart" |

Also broadened `/joplin` — it was titled "Epoxy Floors & Driveway Overlays Joplin Missouri," which is functionally the same query as `/epoxy-floors`, even though the page's actual body content already covers driveways, commercial work, and metallic epoxy for Joplin. Retitled to lead with the broader "concrete contractor" intent the content already supports, so it now differentiates from the epoxy-specific service page instead of duplicating it:
- **Old title:** "Epoxy Floors & Driveway Overlays Joplin Missouri | Crete Creations LLC"
- **New title:** "Concrete Contractor Joplin MO | Epoxy Floors, Driveways & Decorative Concrete | Crete Creations LLC"
- **Old H1:** "Epoxy Floors & Driveways / Joplin, Missouri"
- **New H1:** "Joplin's Concrete / Contractor Since 2000"

No cannibalization was found among the location pages (Carthage, Neosho, Webb City, etc.) — each targets its own city and the titles/content are already unique per page. No cannibalization was found among the 6 educational blog posts (DIY vs. hire, longevity, garage use, installation timeline, epoxy types, surface prep) — each covers a distinct question with a distinct H1.
