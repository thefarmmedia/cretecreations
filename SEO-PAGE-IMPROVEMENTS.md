## Why some pages on the list weren't rewritten

The instruction was to improve pages, not manufacture busywork. Several pages on the priority list were already doing their job correctly: unique content, clear intent, working CTAs, real internal links. Rewriting a page that's already good — just to show "before/after" — would have meant either diluting strong existing copy or introducing the generic-AI-phrasing the brief explicitly said to avoid ("Whether you're looking for...", "Transform your space..."). The actual defects found on this pass were the `/joplin` vs `/epoxy-floors` overlap (fixed) and one dead-end blog post (fixed) — both are now resolved.
